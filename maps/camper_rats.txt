INSTALLATION:

Run Winzip and unzip Camper_Rats.zip.  Open the Camper_Rats folder and place the Camper_Rats.bsp, Camper_Rats.res, and Camper_Rats.txt in your valve/maps folder.   Place the wav files in a folder called camper and place that in your valve/sounds folder.  Also place the Camper_Rats overviews in your valve/overviews folder.  Take the models from the model folder and place the camper model folder in your valve/models folder.

Place the ginger.mdl player model in the valve/models/player/ginger folder.

If you'd like a copy of the camper.wad, email me and I'll send it to you.  docrock64@cox.net
