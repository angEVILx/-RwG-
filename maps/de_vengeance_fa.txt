Vengeance-Bomb Defusel

Original obj_vengeance By Sulsa (Stephen Jackson)

Port by Izuko Fukuda

Background.
     After the destruction of the Chicago-based drug business 
chain by the Chicago SWAT team, Chicago gangsters went on a rampage 
and resolved to retaliate against the Chicago government by launching a t
errorist attack on a monument in downtown Chicago or on the gates of city 
hall.

Counter-Terrorists:
Protect the citizens of Chicago by stopping the 
terrorists from planting their bomb at the city's monument or 
city hall and eliminating all of their members, or, if that fails to 
stop them from planting a bomb at the monument or city hall gates, 
by searching the monument and city hall to locate and 
defuse that bomb.

Tip:Use ladders to climb high above city buildings to 
spy on terrorists on the ground and use sniper rifles to 
assassinate them or use flashbangs.

Terrorists:
Invade downtown Chicago, find and plant your bomb 
at the downtown city monument or city hall gates and 
stop the Counter-Terrorists from defuseing the bomb
and destroying you and your team,or, alternatively, kill the 
Counter-Terrorists before they kill you and your team.

Tip:Use smoke grenades to cover your tracks 
and confuse Counter-Terrorists snipers 
when moving in open areas.

Other:
There are two target locations in downtown Chicago, 
the City Monument and the entrance to City Hall, 
which have been marked with blue buoys.
