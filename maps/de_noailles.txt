Noailles - Bomb/Defuse
Made by Foofinoo

Map and Custom Textures by Foofinoo
Normal Textures by VALVe



There is long time, Terrorists were in a High School...
They doesn't like work and study, then they decided to explode their old High School !!
But the Counter-Terrorists can't allow this action !!

Terrorists : 
Plant the bomb in the Cafeteria or at the base of the High School

Counter-Terrorists :
Protect the High School for all cost



Notes : This map take place in my High School : "Anna de Noailles" at Evian...
I have try to make the most resemblance in real life...

There is the Great Hall, the Cafeteria, the long corridors, the playground...
Some elements are changed (there is not all these buildings...)
And the Leman Lake can be seen from the Great Hall !!
There is some missing buildings... But the most important is here !!
