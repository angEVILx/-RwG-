INSTALLATION:

1. Run Winzip and unzip kitkat_disco.rar

2. Open the maps folder and place the kitkat_disco.bsp, kitkat_disco.res, and kitkat_disco.txt in your valve/maps folder.  

3. Place the 6 (six) cloud9_.tga files into your valve/gfx/env folder.

4. Put the folder called disco with the 1 wav file in it in the valve/sound folder.

5. Put the folder called disco with sprite files in it in the valve/sprites folder.

6. When running the map, open tilde (~) and type mp_allowmonsters.  Press enter.  Then press (~) again and type
changelevel kitkat_disco.  Press enter.  Now the mice and monsters can be seen in the map.
