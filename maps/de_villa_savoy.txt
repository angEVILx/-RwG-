De_Villa_Savoy - Bomb/Defusal
Custom map by I Shoot Rubber Bullets [COF]

Le Corbusier's famous house outside of
Paris, France is in need of remodelling
and some new landscapping.

Terrorists: You have been hired by a Paris
contractor to torch Villa Savoy to collect
insurance money.

CTs: Prevent this architectural jewel from
being demolished.

[Press fire to continue]
