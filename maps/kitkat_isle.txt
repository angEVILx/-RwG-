INSTALLATION:

1. 	Run Winrar and unzip kitkat_isle.zip

2. 	Open the kitkat_isle maps folder and place the kitkat_isle.bsp, kitkat_isle.res, and kitkat_isle.txt in your valve/maps folder.  

3.	Open the gfx/env folder and place the 3dm_bikini.tga files in your valve/gfx/env folder.

4.	Open the kitkat_isle sound folder and put the fever sound folder in your valve/sound folder.

5.	Open the kitkat_isle models folder and place the fever models folder inside your valve/models folder.

6. 	Open the kitkat_isle sprites folder and place the fever sprite folder inside your valve/sprites folder.

7.	Open the kitkat_isle player model folder and place the beachball and bikini model folders in your valve/models/player folder.
