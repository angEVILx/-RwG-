Train Yard - Hostage Rescue
by Cha Siu Fan (glock102020)

Battle in the heart of an industrial rail-transportation centre. Hostages will be held in a train garage.

Terrorists:
The hostages are train drivers that work for a company which delivers medical drugs by rail. Terrorists have held up some drug-filled carriages and are also holding the employees at ransom.

Counter-Terrorists:
Primary objective is to rescue the hostages before they are killed. If possible, clear the warehouse too by eliminating all opposition.

Recommended players: 8 - 12
