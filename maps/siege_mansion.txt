The terrorists have broken into a journalist's home after they were threatened to be exposed to the public. The silent alarm was tripped and the Counter-Terrorists are on their way.

Terrorists: Hide in the shadows and ambush the CTs.
Counter-Terrorists: Eliminate the terrorists.

Other notes: Use the light switches and night vision to your advantage.
