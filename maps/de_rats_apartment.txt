Story: Evil Rats have broken into the landlord's flat and want to make a mess. But the toy soldiers are ready and will fight back....

Rats(Terrorists): Destroy the landlord's computer so he can't continue his work or leave his evening without food by destroying dinner!

Toy Soldiers(Counter-Terrorists): Protect the computer in the workplace in the hall or the dinner in the kitchen from being destroyed!
