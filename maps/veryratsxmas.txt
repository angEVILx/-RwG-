INSTALLATION:

Run Winzip and unzip veryratsxmas.zip.  Open the veryratsxmas folder and place the
veryratsxmas.bsp, veryratsxmas.res, and veryratsxmas.txt in your valve/maps folder.

Place the 6 (six) budcaps.tga files into your valve/gfx/env folder.

Place the 2 wav files in your valve/sound/ambience folder.

Place the wtflare2.spr and the grnflare4 in your valve/sprites folder.

Put the bigfatrat.mdl in your valve/models folder.
