Dead City/City of Damned-Hostages Rescue
Original cs_deadcity_cso by South Korea Nexon
Port by Izuko Fukuda
The abandoned city in Chicago, USA, is slated for 
demolition under the Chicago Urban Renewal Project. However, 
after 4 project leaders vanished without a trace during an on-site 
inspection of the abandoned city, the Chicago government received 
an extortion message from terrorists who had taken control of the 
abandoned city: They have kidnapped the four project leaders and 
are holding them hostage at four separate locations within the 
abandoned city. To secure their safe return, Chicago must surrender 
the abandoned city to them as a base for distributing cocaine across 
the United States and conducting other criminal activities. The FBI 
has already entered the abandoned city.

Counter-Terrorists:
You have breached the Abandoned 
City: Raid and clear the Abandoned City to locate 
the four kidnapped project leaders, or eliminate 
all terrorists within the Abandoned City to reclaim control.

Tip:Abandoned cities feature complex terrain with 
very few cover points: Before moving from one corner to 
another, deploy a Smoke Grenade to obscure the vision 
and hearing of potential terrorist snipers.

Terrorists:
Counter-Terrorists are attempting to enter 
the city and steal the four demolition 
project leaders you have kidnapped: 
Defend the abandoned city and your 
hostages, and eliminate the Counter-Terrorists.

Tip:The abandoned city is vast: use 
this to your advantage by hiding in ambush 
spots the Counter-Terrorists won't expect, gathering 
all audio alerts they emit, and at the right moment, 
throw flashbangs toward the direction the enemy is 
approaching from.

Other Notes:
There are 4 hostages in the abandoned city.
